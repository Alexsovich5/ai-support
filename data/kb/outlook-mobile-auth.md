# Outlook on a phone keeps asking for a password

After a password change, the mail app on a phone prompts for credentials again and again until the account is added again.

1. Remove the work account from the Outlook mobile app.
2. Install the authenticator app and complete multi-factor registration.
3. Add the account again and approve the sign-in request.
4. Check that the phone date and time are set automatically.
5. If mail still does not sync, send the app diagnostics ID to the service desk.
