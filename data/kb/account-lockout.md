# Account locked out

An account locks after several failed sign-in attempts. Old passwords saved on a phone or a mapped drive often cause repeated lockouts.

1. Wait fifteen minutes for the lockout to expire, or ask the service desk to unlock the account.
2. Remove saved credentials for the domain from Credential Manager.
3. Update the password on every phone and tablet that reads work mail.
4. Sign out of any shared workstation where you are still signed in.
5. If the account locks again within the hour, report it so the source of the failed attempts can be traced.
