# Reporting a phishing message

A phishing message tries to trick you into opening an attachment, following a link or giving away a password.

1. Do not click links or open attachments in the suspicious message.
2. Use the Report Phishing button in the mail client.
3. If you entered your password on a linked page, change it at once.
4. Tell the service desk if you opened an attachment.
5. Delete the message after reporting it.
