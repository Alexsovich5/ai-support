# VPN keeps disconnecting

If the VPN client disconnects every few minutes, the tunnel is usually dropped by an unstable home connection or by power saving on the network adapter.

1. Connect the laptop to the router with a cable, or move closer to the access point, and check whether the VPN still disconnects.
2. Open the VPN client, sign out, quit it completely and reconnect.
3. In Device Manager, open the network adapter properties and turn off "Allow the computer to turn off this device to save power".
4. Update the VPN client to the version published on the software portal.
5. If the VPN connection keeps dropping, note the time of each disconnect and send the VPN client log to the service desk.
